Our flat — design ideas

A 2-room flat (≈48 m²): living room with kitchen, bedroom, bathroom and balcony. Work in progress — I'd love to hear what you think!

3D view → Walk around the flat. Start with “From the doorway” to stand in the bathroom door. Floor plan (2D) Exact layout with sizes in metres. Switch bathroom options in the dropdown. Measurement sheet Site measurements behind the plans (mostly for me).

How to use the 3D view

  • Drag to rotate, right-drag (or two fingers) to move, scroll to zoom.
  • Double-click any spot to rotate around it, or use “Center on” to jump to a room.
  • Hover over things to see what they are.
  • “Finish” switches between the green-tile style and a plain view.

What I'd like your opinion on

  • The bathroom: bath along the wall, washer + dryer stacked in the corner, the green tiles + walnut look.
  • Anything that feels cramped, odd, or that you'd do differently.
Sizes are measured on site; heights and socket positions are partly estimates.
